Our kitchen promise

From sourcing to shipping, Pawfect Bites is built on transparency and thoughtful nutrition for every dog we bake for.

Vet-developed recipes

Developed alongside veterinary nutritionists and reviewed quarterly to ensure balanced macros across protein, fat, and fiber.

Clean ingredient lists

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before hitting checkout.

How quickly will my treats arrive?

Orders placed before 2 p.m. EST ship the same day via two-day priority. You’ll receive tracking as soon as your batch leaves our oven.

Do you offer allergy-friendly recipes?

Absolutely. Each season includes at least one recipe free from the top eight canine allergens, and we list all ingredients and possible cross-contact scenarios.

Can I set up a subscription?

Yes! Choose delivery every 2, 4, or 6 weeks. Subscriptions come with rotating chef’s choice samples and can be paused anytime.
