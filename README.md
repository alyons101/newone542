# Pawfect Bites

A single-page storefront for a gourmet dog treat bakery. Browse featured treats, learn about the brand, experiment with a flavor lab, and manage a fully animated shopping cart experience — all in the browser.

## Getting started

1. Open `index.html` in your favorite browser.
2. Explore the hero, brand story, testimonials, and FAQ sections to get to know the bakery.
3. Launch the Kitchen Tour or Flavor Lab modals to see interactive pop-ups with curated content and bundle recommendations.
4. Add treats to the basket to trigger animated toasts, a sliding cart drawer with free-shipping progress, and responsive quantity controls.
5. Click the checkout button to watch the celebratory overlay recap your order and suggest the next collection to explore.

All styling is handled in `styles.css`, and the cart interactions, overlays, and animations are orchestrated in `app.js`.
